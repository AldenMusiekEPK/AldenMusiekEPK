ABOUT

ALDEN is a Pretoria-based indie Afrikaans singer and songwriter who brings a raw, modern sound with strong live energy and a naturally outgoing stage presence. His music is honest, confident, and focused on true audience connection.

Influenced by artists such as Francois van Coke and Spoegwolf, ALDEN balances raw lyrics with contemporary indie influences, carving out a distinctly own voice within the Afrikaans alternative market.

With a commitment to authenticity and musical integrity, ALDEN creates music that resonates on a personal level, capturing the essence of modern South African indie culture through an Afrikaans lens.

GROBLERSDAL

Groblersdal is an indie Afrikaans song focused on the memory of growing up, told from an indirect autobiographical perspective. The lyrics are original, with the story influenced by someone who grew up in Groblersdal, giving the song an honest, observational tone; released on December 11, 2025, the track is a calm, narrative-driven release built on atmosphere and emotion, reflecting ALDEN’s modern, story-focused indie approach.

ARTISTIC VISION

AUTHENTIC CONNECTION

ALDEN's approach to music is rooted in authenticity and genuine audience connection. His performances are characterised by strong live energy and an outgoing stage presence that creates an immediate connection with his listeners.

RAW & MODERN

Balancing raw, honest lyrics with contemporary indie influences, ALDEN brings a fresh perspective to Afrikaans alternative music. His sound is both familiar and distinctly his own, as well as modern without losing touch with authentic storytelling.

CONFIDENT VOICE

With influences from artists like Francois van Coke and Spoegwolf, ALDEN has developed a confident, distinctive voice within the Afrikaans indie scene. His music speaks to a generation seeking honesty and musical integrity in their artists.
